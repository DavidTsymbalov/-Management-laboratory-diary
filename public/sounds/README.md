# Emoji sound cues

Eight original mono PCM WAV cues, generated by `scripts/generate-emoji-sounds.py`.
24 kHz / 16 bit; 0.18–0.40 seconds. Peaks are normalized to 42% full scale;
the player applies another gain reduction. There are no third-party recordings,
Blink application assets or additional runtime sound libraries.

- poop: low wobble and filtered bubble noise
- heart: two heartbeat pulses
- laugh: three soft rising chirps
- fire: short airy crackle
- clap: three decaying hand-clap-like noise pulses
- ghost: descending vibrating tone
- party: pop and three ascending chimes
- zap: fast descending electric chirp
